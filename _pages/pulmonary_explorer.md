<h3>Instructions</h3>

1. Download the .zip folder from this [Google Drive link](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1JbPpD6EtPZ91H4Xzm3qaqvQ5mcw_xEzg/view?usp=drive_link).
2. Unzip the .zip folder.
3. Click on pulmonary_explorer.html, which will open up a web browser.
4. Click on any of the models on the left sidebar.
4. Click and drag to rotate the model, and scroll to zoom in/out.
