### About me

I am a 3rd year PhD candidate in mechanical engineering at Stanford University studying multi-fidelity boundary condition tuning under uncertainty via computational and data-driven methods. I am advised by Dr. Alison Marsden (Stanford). 

As an AHA predoctoral fellow, I am collaborating with Dr. Daniele Schiavazzi (University of Notre Dame) and Dr. Jeffrey Feinstein (Stanford) to develop an automated, uncertainty-aware neural network framework that will provide real-time feedback to clinicians to plan difficult stenting procedures for pediatric patients with peripheral pulmonary arterial stenosis (PPAS).
